Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer Butter Masala is a delicious Indian dish. It is made with paneer, butter, tomatoes, and spices.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Butter
  • Tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Salt and spices
  • Cream

Instructions

  1. Cut the paneer into small pieces.
  2. Chop the tomatoes and onion.
  3. Cook the onion and tomatoes with spices.
  4. Add paneer, butter, and cream.
  5. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.

Important: Serve hot with roti or naan.
